## Current Product Direction

The current recommendation for the Xiaohongshu auto-publishing workflow cards is:

> Independent editorial magazine x content production pipeline

- Base: warm paper, ink black, cool blue-gray
- Accent: vermilion / coral for primary actions and attention states
- Structure: editorial grid, clear baseline, generous whitespace
- Texture: low-opacity paper grain or halftone only where it cannot reduce legibility
- Contrast: strong enough for workflow status and publishing errors

## Most Relevant References

- `GR-01` Minimalist: restrained structure and information-first layout
- `GR-02` Three-Color Hardcut: limited palette and sharp editorial contrast
- `GR-03` Grainy Print: controlled print grain and independent-publication feeling
- `GR-05` Blue-Black Print: blue-black ink, cream paper, quiet handmade editorial tone

## Avoid

- Generic purple-blue SaaS gradients
- Excessive glassmorphism and floating cards
- Multiple competing high-saturation accents
- Decorative noise over titles, statuses, or controls
- Changing the whole visual language from card to card
